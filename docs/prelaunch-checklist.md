# Pre-Launch Checklist (Unresolved Business/Policy Questions)

This checklist tracks unresolved items that should be finalized before production launch. These are intentionally not hard-coded in Phase 1.

## Tax and invoicing
- Confirm GST classification per product type with tax advisor.
- Confirm invoice presentation requirements and GST split display format.
- Confirm treatment for promotions and discounts in tax calculations.
- Confirm any state-specific compliance implications.

## Food compliance labeling
- Confirm mandatory product labels for online sale (e.g., FSSAI and packaging fields) with legal/compliance advisor.
- Confirm which fields must appear on PDP, checkout, invoice, and shipment docs.

## Shipping operations
- Define serviceability policy by destination and courier service class.
- Define fallback behavior when no quote is available.
- Define quote validity window and customer messaging for expired quotes.
- Define escalation and manual intervention for repeated booking failures.

## Customer policy and after-sales
- Finalize cancellation eligibility windows by order/shipment status.
- Finalize return eligibility criteria and evidence requirements.
- Finalize refund timelines and communication commitments.
- Finalize partial refund policy and approval workflow.
- Finalize customer support SLAs and contact channels.

## Legal and policy governance
- Record policy owners and approvers.
- Record policy effective dates.
- Define change management process for policy updates.
